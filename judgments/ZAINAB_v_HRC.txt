RATIO DECIDENDI  1. CONTRACT: DEFINITION AND VALIDITY OF A CONTRACT: Let me start by defining contract. A contract is an agreement between two or more persons which creates an obligation to do or not to do a particular thing. Its essentials are competent parties, subject matter, a legal consideration, mutuality of agreement and mutuality of obligation. See BILANTE INTERNATIONAL LIMITED V NDIC (2011) 15 NWLR (Pt. 1270) 407. For a contract to be birthed, certain elements must be present. These are Offer, Acceptance, Consideration, Intention to create legal relations and Capacity. An offer serve as a foundation in upon which other elements are built. Hence, it is important define an offer. An offer is a definite understanding made with the intention that it shall become binding on the person making it, as soon it is accepted by the person to whom it is addressed. Hence, there must be an offer before acceptance. See EL-HANAN VENTURES LTD. V. RAYFIELD MEDICAL SERVICES LTD (2021) 10 NWLR (Pt. 1784) 211 CA.   2. CONTRACT: NOVATION OF CONTRACT With respect to Novation as argued by the claimant, did the promise to pay the sum of #10,000 to the winner of the competition by the defendant alter the contract? The answer is in the negative. CW2 herself testified before this honourable court that she joined the competition for the purpose of Knowledge. Also CW1 testified that their Trainer, one Shittu Olamide told the group members that the sum promised has no legal effect. Let me quickly add that the evidence of a witness against a party who has called him is admissible and is weighty. See LERIC NIG. LTD V. U.B.N. LTD (2000) 12 SCNJ 184 and the case of SHITTU OLAMIDE V THE ATTORNEY GENERAL, THE HUMAN RIGHT CHAMBERS LSSJ/003/21. Subsequently, DW1 in his written statement on oath particularly in paragraph 5 stated that he was a member of group 5. This was the same group the claimant belonged to. He testified before this honourable court that the defendant through it’s agent, promised the sum of #10,000 but it was rebutted on the same day as the statement was merely to encourage other group members who were not serious with the training. My Lords, it is evident that the testimony of CW1, CW2, and DW1 coupled with the statement on oath of DW1 points towards a singular direction, which is that no new contract was formed by way of Novation. The initial contract was an offer to train for and participate in a beginner’s moot competition which the claimant accepted. There was no Novation in this instant case. The previous contract was not altered, and parties to the contract discharged their duties appropriately. 3. CONTRACT: OFFER- WHAT IS AN OFFER Hence, it is important to define an offer. CONTRACT: WHEN IS A CONTRACT FORMED A contract is formed once there is an offer by the offeror to the offeree which is accepted by the offeree backed by consideration. At that point in time, the parties to the contract are said to be ad idem or in agreement, and that agreement or contract is binding on both parties and as such it is enforceable by action. See MEKWUNYE V. WEST AFRICAN EXAMINATION COUNCIL (2020) 6 NWLR (Pt. 1719) 1 SC. 7. COURT: ORDER OF SPECIFIC PERFORMANCES AND DAMAGES My Lords, permit me to importantly add that an order of specific performance is entirely different from damages. While the former is an equitable remedy, the latter is a common law remedy. Damages are compensation for loss, injury or harm of any kind. See SHELL PETROLEUM DEV. CO. OF NIGERIA LTD.V. KATAD NIGERIA LTD. (2006) 1 NWLR (Pt. 960) 198 CA. However, A decree or an order of specific performance is a form of relief that is purely equitable in origin, and the fundamental rule is that specific performance will not be decreed or ordered if there is an absolute remedy at law in answer to the plaintiff’s claim. For instance, where the plaintiff would be adequately compensated by the common law remedy of damages. The jurisdiction in specific performance is therefore anchored on the inadequacy of the remedy of damages at law. EZENWA V OKO (2008) 3 NWLR (Pt. 1075) 610 SC. 8. On the 25th of August, 2022, the defendant notified the general public of the Law Students’ Society, faculty of law, University of Ilorin through its WhatsApp media platform that it has begun the registration for participation in its “Human rights chambers beginners moot competition & training” wherein the competition proper would take place from the 10th of September till the 11th September,2022.
